# Gravity Gate

Created: 2026-06-07

Purpose: decide whether gravity is part of the present CHO framework or remains an exploratory side project.

## Gate Question

Phase 5 asks whether the kinematic metric brick in `compute/gravity_curvature.py` can be promoted to a real gravity sector.

A pass would require all of the following in one coherent construction:

1. a 4D Lorentzian metric,
2. a transformation law under `SO(3,1)`,
3. a flat limit,
4. a candidate dynamics or variational principle with a Newton constant or Einstein-like field equation.

The executable gate is `compute/gravity_gate_audit.py`.

## What Still Works

The earlier M-GRAV brick survives.

`compute/gravity_curvature.py` builds the internal associator metric

```text
g_(mu nu)(a,b) = <[e_mu,a,b], [e_nu,a,b]>
```

on `Im(O)`. It is symmetric, positive semidefinite, `G2`-covariant, rank `4`, transverse to the source bivector, and has flat directions equal to the associative quaternionic subalgebra generated by `a` and `b`.

The Phase 5 gate rechecks the key signature fact:

```text
signature on Im(O): (4 positive, 0 negative, 3 zero)
```

That is a strong kinematic result, but it is not Lorentzian gravity.

The separate `C x H ~= M_2(C)` arena also works:

```text
det(X) = x0^2 - x1^2 - x2^2 - x3^2,
SL(2,C): X -> A X A^dagger
```

so the flat Lorentzian spacetime arena exists. The problem is joining it to the octonionic curvature without extra structure.

## Main Negative Result

The gate tests whether `G2` symmetry selects a canonical four-plane inside the seven-dimensional internal space `Im(O)`.

Using the finite signed-permutation automorphism witness of `G2`:

```text
|G2_finite| = 1344
commutant dimension = 1
```

The group-average of any rank-four projector becomes

```text
P_avg = (4/7) I_7
```

with eigenvalues

```text
[4/7, 4/7, 4/7, 4/7, 4/7, 4/7, 4/7].
```

This is not a projector. It means the available `G2` symmetry washes out a chosen four-plane rather than selecting one.

A hand-picked four-plane also does not solve the signature problem: restricting the internal associator metric to a coordinate four-plane remains positive semidefinite, with no time direction.

## Flat Limit

The gate does find a clean perturbative flat limit. If the source bivector area goes to zero, then

```text
tr g = 16 sin^2(theta)
```

and the fitted scaling is

```text
tr g ~ theta^1.999.
```

So the internal curvature turns off smoothly. This is useful, but it is not the Newtonian weak-field limit: there is no `1/r` potential, field equation, or Newton constant.

## Verdict

```text
AUDIT STATUS: PASS
GRAVITY STATUS: OUT OF SCOPE FOR THE PRESENT FRAMEWORK
```

The gravity line remains an interesting kinematic research line, not a derived sector of the current theory.

## Why No `gravity_lorentz_reduction.py`

The earlier scope review asked for `compute/gravity_lorentz_reduction.py` only if the Lorentzian reduction was viable.

The gate shows it is not viable yet:

- no `G2`-invariant four-plane is selected;
- the internal metric is positive semidefinite, not Lorentzian;
- the `C x H` Lorentzian arena and `O` curvature are not joined;
- no dynamics or Newton constant emerges.

So no reduction module is created. The executable artifact is instead the decision gate `compute/gravity_gate_audit.py`.
